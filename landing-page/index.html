What is the area of a triangle with base 8cm and height 5cm?
Area = ½ × base × height
= ½ × 8 × 5 = 20 cm²
Explain in Hindi?
त्रिभुज का क्षेत्रफल = ½ × आधार × ऊंचाई
= ½ × 8 × 5 = 20 सेमी²
