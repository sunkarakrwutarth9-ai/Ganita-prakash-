Lines and Angles

CHAPTER 03

Number Play

CHAPTER 04

Data Handling and Presentation

Prime Time

CLASS VII

15 NCERT Class 7 Chapters

CHAPTER 01

Large Numbers Around Us

Exploring lakhs, crores, place value, patterns in products, and real-world applications of big numbers.

CHAPTER 02

Arithmetic Expressions

Understanding and evaluating expressions, order of operations, and reading complex mathematical phrases.

CHAPTER 03

A Peek Beyond the Point

Introduction to decimals - tenths, hundredths, place value, units of measurement, and arithmetic with decimals.

CHAPTER 04

Expressions Using Letter-Numbers

Algebraic thinking - using letters to represent unknowns, forming and simplifying expressions.

CHAPTER 05

Parallel and Intersecting Lines

Intersecting lines, perpendicular lines, parallel lines, transversals, and corresponding angles.

CHAPTER 06

Number Play

Number patterns, parity, grid explorations, Virahanka-Fibonacci sequence, and digit puzzles.

CHAPTER 07

A Tale of Three Intersecting Lines

Properties of triangles - equilateral triangles, construction, altitudes, and types of triangles.

CHAPTER 08

Working with Fractions

Multiplication and division of fractions with real-world applications.

CHAPTER 09

Geometric Twins

Understanding congruence - congruent figures, congruence of triangles, and criteria for congruence.

CHAPTER 10

Operations with Integers

Recap of integers, multiplication and division of positive and negative integers.

CHAPTER 11

Finding Common Ground

Highest Common Factor (HCF) and Lowest Common Multiple (LCM) - properties and applications.

CHAPTER 12

Another Peek Beyond the Point

Multiplication and division of decimals - extending operations with real-world applications.

CHAPTER 13

Connecting the Dots

Statistical thinking - collecting data, representative values (mean, median, mode), and interpretation.

CHAPTER 14

Constructions and Tilings

Geometric constructions, perpendicular bisectors, angle bisectors, and tessellation patterns.

CHAPTER 15

Finding the Unknown

Introduction to equations - balancing scales, setting up equations, and solving systematically.
